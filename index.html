Privacy Policy

Lumberjack Valley · last updated 25 September 2026

Short version: this game collects nothing, sends nothing and shares nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network requests of any kind.

Information we collect

None. Lumberjack Valley does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and the game contains no account system, no login and no advertising.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress — your money, upgrade levels, unlocked areas and hired workers — using your device's local storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not readable by us or by anyone else;
  • contains no personal information;
  • is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. Everything it needs is included in the download. It works with the device in airplane mode. The mobile app declares the standard internet permission only because the packaging framework (Capacitor) uses it to serve the game's own files from inside the app itself.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no crash reporters, no social logins, no third-party software development kits.

The web version on game portals

Everything above describes the app you install from an app store. The same game is also playable in a web browser on game portals such as CrazyGames. That web version runs inside the portal's page and uses the portal's own software development kit for three things: to save your progress to your portal account when you are signed in, to tell the portal when the game is loading or paused, and to show the portal's advertising — an optional rewarded video you may choose to watch for a bonus, and a short ad when you choose to be reborn. Any data handled in that process is handled by the portal under its own privacy policy, not by us. We still collect nothing ourselves.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, no data about children is collected either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date at the top. Since the game has no network access, changes will only ever accompany an app update.
